================================================================================
- Step 0a: exercise names are cleaned — the dataset's "30 …" / "30 Day …" prefix (with its body-part word) and a stray
  trailing dash are gone from the name shown. 198 of 2,893 names; ids, slugs and the original title are untouched.
- Step 0b: the same movement with different equipment is now ONE exercise family with variants. 2,893 exercises →
  2,181 families (192 with 2+ variants). One primary exercise per family + variant (2,481); the 412 other copies keep
  their ids for old plans / logs / PRs and are offered nowhere except "Show all" search and the family card.
- Every family is tagged with movement patterns (26) from the exercise name first, then body part and type. 141
  exercises (132 families) are unclear; the coach's review list has 197 families.
- The AI draft became a guided flow: one call returns a "Training summary" (who she is, experience, points to check),
  1–3 split options with pros / cons from the split config, and the full plan for option 1 — every exercise in a slot,
  as a family + a variant she has the equipment for. All of it is checked in code and retried with its problems.
- The coach chooses the path in the dashboard: use the full AI plan · use the split only (typed workouts, empty slots,
  the AI's picks offered per slot) · regenerate (AI chooses, or for any split). A manual path does the same without AI:
  "+ Add workout" with a workout type, a slot-filtered picker, and a variant switch in the exercise card.
- New: an Exercises screen in the dashboard (review list, patterns, primary, merge / split families) and one optional
  onboarding question (training experience) on the frequency step.

3. DECISIONS AND HOW THEY WERE BUILT
================================================================================
Owner answers (2026-10-09) are written into PRD §8 "Decisions — Phase 12b" and BUILD_PLAN "Phase 12b".

0a — names (exercises/exerciseName.ts, `npm run clean:exercise-names`)
- One pure function, used by the importer, the migration and the tests. The name is rebuilt from
  source.originalTitle, so running it again changes nothing. The slug stays derived from the original title.
- Only the number 30 is treated as the program prefix ("180 Jump", "3/4 sit-up", "90/90 …", "6 Way …" stay).
  This was tightened after the first run: the first version would have stripped any leading number from a NEW name;
  the 198 renames are identical before and after the fix.

0b — families (features/exerciseFamilies, `npm run group:exercises`; also runs after import:exercises)
- Key = movement words (source prefixes like HM / FYR2 / Holman / King Maker, equipment words, "weighted",
  plural / hyphen spellings removed) + movement patterns + library type. The body part is NOT in the key (owner
  decision): "Barbell Deadlift" filed under hamstrings, lower back and middle back is one family, and the family
  keeps all those body parts.
- Kept apart and put on the review list: the same name with different patterns (34), and names without a pattern
  that appear under several body parts (19) — there the name alone is not trusted.
- Variant = the equipment word in the name when there is one (Smith machine is filed as "machine" or "barbell" in
  the dataset), else the library equipment.
- Primary per family + variant: the coach's chosen primary → an exercise she already used in a plan → the plainest
  name. The family name drops the equipment word only when the family really has several variants ("Romanian
  Deadlift"), otherwise the full name stays ("Kettlebell Swing").
- Coach edits are never overwritten by a re-run: exercises she moved (merge / split), family names and patterns she
  changed, primaries she chose. Covered by a test.

Movement patterns (exercises/exercisePatterns.ts)
- Name rules first (the dataset's body part is often wrong: "Leg Extension" under hamstrings, deadlifts under middle
  back), then generic words guarded by the body part ("press" on chest / shoulders), then a body-part fallback
  (biceps, calves, abs …). No pattern, or a combo of 3+ patterns = unclear → review list.
- An unreviewed unclear family is never offered in a slot or to the AI (found in the browser check: a combo like
  "180 Jump to Renegade Side Raise" was showing in the horizontal-pull slot).

Split config (features/trainingSplits/trainingSplit.config.ts + TrainingSettings per coach)
  Splits:        full body 1–4 days · upper / lower 2, 4 · upper / lower / full body 3 · upper / lower + glute focus
                 3, 4 · push / pull / legs 3, 6 · PPL + upper / lower 5 · ABC 3, 6 · ABC + glute focus 3, 6
                 (7 days is planned as 6).
  Workout types: full body · upper · lower · lower – glute focus · push · pull · legs · ABC A / B / C.
  ABC = A chest + back, B legs, C shoulders + arms — marked `coachToConfirm` in the config; the note for the coach is
  docs/reference/abc-split-question.md (Hebrew + English; local, gitignored like the rest of docs/reference).
  Overrides are stored and merged (GET /api/coach/training/config, PUT /api/coach/training/settings); the settings
  SCREEN is in the backlog (PRD §14, BUILD_PLAN backlog).

AI (prompt version training-plan-v3)
- One call: summary, experienceRead, considerations, splitOptions (as many as fit her days, max 3), and the workouts
  of option 1. Split / workout type / slot ids come from the coach's config; families are aliases F001…F200; the
  variant must be one listed for that family (only variants her equipment and level allow are listed).
- Checked in code, problems sent back (max 3 attempts): split ids, day counts, option count, no duplicate option,
  1–3 pros and cons, option 1 = the coach's split when she asked for one, the day pattern, slot belongs to the
  workout type, the family's patterns fit the slot, the variant, required slots filled, max per slot — plus every
  Phase 12 rule (the dashboard's own save + publish rules, one day per workout, no exercise twice, still in the
  library). "5–5" reps are stored as a fixed 5.
- Regenerate for a split: POST …/ai-recommendations { type, splitId }. A split outside her days uses its nearest
  day count.
- "Prefers low impact" removes power (plyometric / Olympic) families in code.
- Experience: `new` / `some` / no answer → beginner + intermediate; `experienced` → + advanced (the library has only
  13 advanced exercises, so the answer matters mostly for the split and volume).

What changed in the Phase 12 flow and data
- New optional fields only: Plan.splitId, workout.workoutType, exercise.slotId (coach-only — added in the coach's
  plan view, never in the trainee's), Exercise.familyId / variant / isPrimaryVariant / familySource,
  AiRecommendation.request, answers.trainingExperience. New collections: ExerciseFamily, TrainingSettings.
- Existing plans have no types / slots and look and behave as before. "Duplicate last week" copies types and slots.
- Existing AI drafts (v1 / v2) still open: the card shows them as one plan with "Use in a new plan", no options.
- The side exercise library in the builder now lists families (search, pattern, level) instead of 2,893 rows.
- Removed: the AiDraftCard component (replaced by TrainingSummaryCard); the per-muscle candidate selection.

Things I decided inside your answers (say if you want any changed)
- A slot holds at most 2 exercises by default (config).
- In a typed workout the exercises stay grouped by slot; dragging reorders inside a slot.
- A pick from the picker adds the first variant she can do and opens the variant chips in the row — one click to
  swap. Swapping keeps sets, reps, rest, notes and the slot.
- "Use split only" links the plan to the AI draft, so the rationale shows and the AI's picks appear as suggestions
  in the empty slots (only when the plan is for the split the AI planned).
- The picker's typo search is the shared one (one typo per word, like the food picker).
- Family edits are global (the library is shared) — fine with one coach; with several coaches they need to become
  per-coach.

AI output text check (added 2026-10-11, owner's request before committing)
- aiRecommendations/aiOutputText.ts `findForeignTextIssues`: walks every text in the parsed reply; allowed = Hebrew
  and Latin letters plus Unicode's script-neutral characters (digits, punctuation, symbols, spaces, emoji and their
  joiners / accents). Letters or digits of any other script (Thai, Cyrillic, Arabic, Chinese …) are a problem.
- It runs in the generic runner (aiRecommendation.service.ts), next to each kind's own validation, so training,
  nutrition and any later kind get it. Its sentences join the kind's issues, go back to the model and count toward
  the same 3 attempts; each names the field ("splitOptions[0].why"), the characters and the text. At most 5 are
  listed per attempt. No prompt change (the prompt version stays training-plan-v3).
